# Privacy Policy for Still

**Effective Date:** August 22, 2026  
**Publisher:** Origin Ark Studio  
**Application:** Still (`com.originark.still`)

Still is designed from the ground up with a privacy-by-design, listen-first architecture. We believe your focus and thought process belong solely to you.

---

### 1. Microphone & Audio Processing
- **Focus Session Only:** The microphone is activated *only* when you explicitly initiate a Focus Session. There is zero background audio recording, ambient monitoring, or 24/7 listening.
- **On-Device Memory Parsing:** Audio input captured during active focus sessions is parsed ephemerally on-device to determine speech activity and prevent nudges while you are speaking. 
- **No Cloud Uploads or Storage:** Audio recordings are never recorded to disk, never uploaded to any remote server, and never shared with third parties.

### 2. Local Data Storage
- **Tasks and Session Logs:** All intention task titles, notes, and focus session timestamps are stored locally on your device via an encrypted SQLite/Room database.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need an account, email address, or third-party login to use Still.

### 3. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing
- Still uses Google Play Billing to manage subscriptions for premium voice presets (`still_voices_monthly` and `still_voices_yearly`).
- All financial transactions and payment instrument details are processed exclusively by Google Play in accordance with the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/about/play-terms/). Still never sees or stores your payment details.

### 4. Network Security & Cleartext Traffic
- Still enforces `usesCleartextTraffic="false"`. No unencrypted network communication is permitted.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Still does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

### 6. Contact Us
For questions regarding Still or this Privacy Policy, contact Origin Ark Studio via our repository or developer channels.
