## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Still is a quiet, listen-first focus companion designed for deep work, study, and intentional living.

Unlike conventional productivity apps that disrupt your flow with incessant pings, Still respects your mental space. It listens gently during your active focus sessions and speaks rarely—offering a single, grounding one-sentence reminder of your intention only when you drift, with a hard-locked minimum interval of 8 minutes between nudges.

### Why Still?

🌿 **Listen-First, Speak-Rarely Architecture**  
Still prioritizes listening over talking. If you're talking or actively in flow, Still stays silent. When nudging is appropriate, it delivers a single calm, serene sentence to guide you back to your started task.

🔒 **Focus Session Only Mic (Never 24/7)**  
Your privacy is sacred. The microphone is only ever enabled during an explicit, user-initiated focus session. Audio is parsed strictly on-device in memory and is never recorded, stored, or sent to external servers.

⏱️ **8-Minute Minimum Hard Lock**  
Still never pesters you. Nudges enforce an uncompromising 8-minute minimum cooldown to preserve cognitive stillness.

📝 **On-Device Intention & Task Management**  
Set your core task for the session. Still anchors you to what matters most. Everything is stored locally on your device with zero cloud tracking.

🎙️ **Serene TTS Voice Presets**  
- **Still Calm (Free):** Default peaceful, grounding cadence.
- **Forest Whisper (Subscription):** Soft, nature-inspired timber with low resonance.
- **Dusk Serenity (Subscription):** Warm twilight tone to ease tension.
- **Zen Harbor (Subscription):** Steady, reassuring voice for deep immersive sessions.

🎨 **Calming Design**  
Designed with Material 3 in pure dark mode accented exclusively by calming Sage (`#718B5F`).
